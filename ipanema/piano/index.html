Left hand: root Right hand: 3rd Right hand: 7th Hollow ring (bar by bar) = where that finger was in the chord before Left keyboard = left hand, right keyboard = right hand (starts at middle C). Number on a key = finger (1 thumb … 5 pinky). Small word under it = which note of the chord.

How to play it

  1. Each chord is just 3 keys: one note in the left hand, two in the right.
  2. Press all three at the start of each bar and let them ring. That's the whole part.
  3. The right hand barely moves. Each bar tells you how many right-hand keys change.
  4. Once that's easy, play the right hand again in the middle of the bar for a bossa feel.
